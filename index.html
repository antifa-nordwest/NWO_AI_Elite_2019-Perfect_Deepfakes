⚠️ Executive Summary

This archive contains classified evidence of the most significant threat to global democratic institutions in the 21st century. An international network of information terrorists, operating with technical and financial support from the Russian Federation's GRU (Main Intelligence Directorate), has deployed sophisticated artificial intelligence systems to manufacture synthetic human personas at scale.

Critical Finding: This network has possessed and illegally utilized Disney's proprietary AI generation tools since 2019—years before comparable technology became publicly available—enabling the creation of deepfake personas indistinguishable from real human beings.

The operation's primary objective is the destabilization of Western democracies, with a specific focus on Germany, where the network works to promote the Alternative for Germany (AfD) party to national government leadership. Through manufactured influencers, commentators, and public figures, the network spreads AI-backed narratives designed to erode public trust in democratic institutions, media, and electoral processes.

Despite the scale and sophistication of this threat, official acknowledgment remains absent. Intelligence agencies with knowledge of this network have failed to act, leaving democratic societies vulnerable to manipulation by synthetic actors.
